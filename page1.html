What is the BDR Conspiracy?

A broken and confusing tradition requiring decisions from first victims, Josephine and Raynisha. The Tradition around Oct. 31st, halloween. They call it “BDR’s arrival”. The tactic of the two victims create submitted letters from “BDR” and hand them out individually to future/possible victims during a 30 minute break. The letters contain the possibility of them being the chosen victim of BDR’s this year, a note containing something that has to do with their personal life and an eerie signature. The final part of this tradition is a final letter revealing who the BDR victim is.
